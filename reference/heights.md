# University Student Heights

Heights of 100 randomly selected male university students from Table 2.1
of Spiegel and Stephens (2008).

## Usage

``` r
heights
```

## Format

### 

An `integer vector` with length `100`.

- heights:

  Height of the student in inches.

## Source

Adapted from Spiegel, Murray R., and Larry J. Stephens, (2008). *Theory
and Problems of Statistics*. 4th edn. McGraw-Hill.
